axi-soc-versal-core Documentation
=================================

AXI-Lite, AXI-Stream, DMA, and AI Engine platform library for SLAC Versal
SoC designs, including the shared Yocto build, used as a ``git submodule``
by downstream firmware projects.  AI Engine support is optional per
board: it is used on AIE-capable devices such as the VEK280 and left out
on devices without an AI Engine array, such as the VRK160 and VRK165.
